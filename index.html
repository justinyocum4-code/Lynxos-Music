Import from Google Takeout

Bring your YouTube Music likes and playlists into this app. Google can't sign in inside apps, so this uses a free export file instead — no password needed here.

Steps: 1. Go to takeout.google.com. 2. Tap "Deselect all", then check "YouTube and YouTube Music". 3. Tap "All YouTube data included", tap "Deselect all", check "playlists" (and "music library" if you see it), then OK. 4. "Next step", then "Create export". Google emails you a download link when it's ready — usually minutes, sometimes hours. 5. Download the zip and unzip it (your Files app can do this). 6. Come back here, tap "Choose files", open the playlists folder, select every CSV file, then tap Import.

About

Lynxos Music v2.2.139. Plays through the app's built-in player — strictly music, no interviews or talk videos — and keeps playing with the screen off. Home has your mixes and new releases from your favorite artists, Explore has genre stations, and your Library keeps songs, albums, playlists, and artists separate. Any album page has a Save album button, and every Home row has a See all button for a full vertical list. The YouTube Music button sends a song to your YouTube Music app.
